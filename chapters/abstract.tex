%\textit{``Non-Invasive Monitoring System for the Elderly Using Artificial Vision and Adaptive Behavioral Learning''}

%Population aging generates a growing demand for solutions that allow family members to stay informed about the condition and daily routine of elderly relatives in their care, especially when distance or daily obligations prevent continuous presence. Most existing systems require the elderly person to wear a device or actively interact with technology, which limits their effectiveness in emergency situations.

%This project proposes the development of a fully non-invasive monitoring system for elderly individuals in domestic environments, based on artificial vision and adaptive behavioral learning. The system detects falls, prolonged immobility, and activities of daily living, through real-time video analysis using pose estimation models. Additionally, it continuously builds a personalized behavioral baseline for each individual, enabling the identification of changes in habitual behavioral patterns.

%Video processing is performed locally on a controller unit, ensuring user privacy, and only detected events are transmitted to a mobile application to notify family members. The system was validated in a controlled domestic environment, demonstrating the technical feasibility of the proposed approach.
